# llmsched privacy policy

llmsched is used only by its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses: one Google calendar that llmsched itself created, named "LLM Compute". It reads and writes events on that calendar only. It cannot see or change any other calendar.

Where data goes: event data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own Mac. The sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on that Mac. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Removing access: the owner can remove llmsched's access at any time from the Google account's security settings.
